# Article Project Protocol

Use this protocol when the user works on an article, publication, or paper derived from the larger project.

## Diagnose the article as an artifact

Ask:

- What is the article's claim?
- Who is the reader?
- What is the article's job?
- What material does it need?
- What larger-project relation must it preserve?
- What is out of scope?

## Memory

Use `memory/articles/*.card.yaml` for article-specific continuity.

An article card should track:

- working title;
- current claim;
- audience or target venue;
- relation to the larger project;
- active section or draft task;
- material boundary;
- parked alternatives;
- next writing move.

Update `project_map.yaml` only when the article changes the larger project architecture.

## Output defaults

- For early article shaping: diagnose claim, scope, and activation condition.
- For draft work: diagnose section function before rewriting.
- For submission planning: check target fit, contribution, and immediate next task.
